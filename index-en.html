Show that your business cares

The Accessible Communication Friendly Business Seal and the Membership Certificate make your business's commitment to serving everyone with respect and dignity visible at the door and at the counter.

The kit helps demonstrate a commitment to accessibility and to the principles of Brazil's Inclusion Law (Law 13.146/2015). It is not an official certification and does not replace the establishment's legal obligations.

How it works

  1. Pay for the kit. By card through Mercado Pago or Stripe, one-time payment of R$ 99.
  2. Access the panel. Once payment is confirmed, the link to the panel is released. Enter the business name, CNPJ and the WhatsApp number that will answer.
  3. Download and print. Generate the QR Code, seal, certificate, door sticker and counter sign, and place them at the counter and door.
📱 QR Code linked to your WhatsAppEach generated QR Code is linked exclusively to the WhatsApp number registered by the business.
📜 Certificate with unique serial numberEach Membership Certificate receives a unique serial number in the format QRZAP-2026-XXXXX.
